# Sample sources

| Instrument | Source | License | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| kalimba | [Versilian Community Sample Library](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL), *Idiophones / Plucked Idiophones / Kalimba, Tanzania* | CC0 1.0 | Real recordings. Ten pitches, fetched and converted by `tools/fetch_vcsl.py`. |

VCSL is CC0, so it carries no conditions at all; the credit above is a
courtesy, not an obligation.

Anything else recorded that lands in `src/Samples.h` needs a license
compatible with GPL-3.0-or-later — CC0 and CC-BY work, anything
non-commercial does not. Record the origin, the license and the date here
before embedding it.

`tools/render_kalimba.py` remains in the repository as a synthesized
fallback and as a way to prototype an instrument no recording exists for.
